4 rules for when practice runs late and the kids are starving.

Swipe for the rules

Dad Fuel2/6
Rule 01

Protein first

Order thisGrilled chicken, or double the meat

Build the order around protein, then fill in the rest. It keeps you fuller longer.

Dad tip: Decide before you pull up. The menu board is not your friend.

Dad Fuel3/6
Rule 02

Don't drink it

SwapSoda for water, diet, or unsweet tea

Liquid calories don't fill you up. A large soda can cost as much as a side.

Dad tip: Easiest win on this list. Zero willpower required.

Dad Fuel4/6
Rule 03

Kid-size the fries

SwapLarge fries for small, or a side salad

You don't have to skip them. Just stop upsizing them.

Dad tip: Stealing 3 from your kid's bag counts. Keep it to 3.

Dad Fuel5/6
Rule 04

Sauce on the side

Ask forSauce and dressing on the side

Dip, don't drown. You get the flavor and skip most of the extra calories.

Dad tip: One sauce cup. Not four.
