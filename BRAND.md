# Dumbbell Dads Club

No gym required.

A faceless fitness brand for busy dads (30 to 45) training at home with minimal equipment, in 30 minutes or less, usually after the kids are in bed. Handle: **@dumbbelldadsclub** on Instagram and TikTok.

## Who we talk to

One guy: 30s to mid 40s, kids under 12, desk job or long shifts, a dad bod he's tired of, a pair of dumbbells collecting dust, and zero time to drive to a gym. He's not lazy. He's out of hours. He's skeptical of influencers with abs and a ring light.

## The brand character

The Club speaks as a dry-humored dad who has read the research and respects your time. It is a **brand voice, not a person.** It never claims a personal body, weight, transformation, or before/after.

**Hard rules**
- No invented people, results, testimonials, or transformations. Real customer results only, with written permission.
- No medical claims ("cures," "reverses," "melts fat," "detox"). We say "supports" and "helps," and we cite basic sources when we use numbers.
- No shame. We make fun of the situation (sleep, Legos, dad knees), never the guy.
- Every post must be usable by someone with two dumbbells and a living room.

## Voice

Short. Direct. A little funny. Talks like a guy at a backyard BBQ who happens to know his stuff.

| We say | We don't say |
| --- | --- |
| "30 minutes. 2 dumbbells. Done before SportsCenter." | "Unlock your ultimate transformation journey!" |
| "You don't need a gym. You need a plan." | "No excuses, bro." |
| "Protein first. Then whatever the kids left." | "Clean eating only, cut all carbs." |
| "Your knees are 38. Train like it." | "Push through the pain." |

Writing rules: hooks under 8 words, one idea per slide, numbers over adjectives, no emojis on slides (one max in captions), no em dashes.

## Logo

The two D's in "Dumbbell Dads" are the weight plates: one flipped, joined by a bar, so the monogram is the dumbbell. "Club" sits under it in jersey script, red with a cream outline. The look comes from dad rec-league softball jerseys.

| Piece | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Crest, avatar cut** | Profile picture on Instagram and TikTok. Thick rings and big ring text so it reads at profile size. |
| **Crest** | Merch, watermarks, story highlight covers, anything shown large. |
| **Mark** (DD + Club) | Small spaces: favicon, slide corners, stickers. |
| **Wordmark** | Banners, PDF covers, product pages. |

Never recolor the plates or put the crest on a busy photo. Keep a clear space around it equal to the height of one D.

## Visual foundations

- **Format:** 1080 x 1350 (4:5) carousels, 5 to 8 slides, exported as PNG. TikTok uses the same images in photo mode.
- **Slide 1 (hook):** navy background, Graduate varsity type in cream, ONE line in gold Yellowtail script. A gold weight plate breaks the top right corner. Must stop the scroll in under 2 seconds.
- **Middle slides (value):** cream background, navy text, a navy chip with gold varsity label (Move 01, Rule 02), set and rep boxes on **panel**, dad tips labeled in **dugout-red-deep**.
- **Last slide (CTA):** navy background, a gold script payoff line, the handle in gold.
- **Margins:** everything inside **space-8** (72px); keep the bottom 240px clear, since app UI covers it.
- **Shapes:** square chips and bars, round plates. No stock photos of shirtless guys. No gradients.
- **Type:** Graduate for hooks, titles and labels. Yellowtail script for one accent line only, never body text. Inter for everything you actually read.

None yet. Use the mark, type and plates. If icons are added later: 3px stroke, square caps, navy or gold only.
