Upper body.
20 minutes.
Living room.

5 moves. 2 dumbbells. Done before the dishwasher finishes.

Swipe for the workout

The Workout2/7
Move 01

Push-up

3 x 10sets x reps

Hands under shoulders, body in one straight line. Chest to the floor, then push it away.

Dad tip: Too hard? Hands on the couch. Still counts.

The Workout3/7
Move 02

Bent-over row

3 x 12sets x reps

Hinge forward, flat back, a dumbbell in each hand. Pull both to your hips and squeeze.

Dad tip: Pretend you are starting two lawn mowers. Slowly.

The Workout4/7
Move 03

Seated press

3 x 10sets x reps

Sit tall on a chair. Press both dumbbells overhead. Lower to shoulder height.

Dad tip: Sitting keeps your low back out of it.

The Workout5/7
Move 04

Hammer curl

3 x 12sets x reps

Palms facing each other. Curl up, elbows pinned to your sides. No swinging.

Dad tip: If your hips move, the weight is too heavy.

The Workout6/7
Move 05

Floor skull crusher

3 x 12sets x reps

On your back, dumbbells over your chest. Bend only at the elbows, lower beside your head, press back up.

Dad tip: Go light. It is called a skull crusher for a reason.
