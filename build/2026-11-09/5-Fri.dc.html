Swipe for the moves

The Workout2/7
Move 01

Cat-cow

8slow reps

On hands and knees. Round your back up, then let it sag and look up. Move slowly.

Dad tip: Breathe out as you round. Breathe in as you look up.

The Workout3/7
Move 02

World's greatest stretch

5each side

Big lunge step, back knee down, hand inside your front foot. Reach the other arm to the ceiling.

Dad tip: The name is not an exaggeration.

The Workout4/7
Move 03

Goblet squat hold

30 secx 2

Hold one dumbbell at your chest. Sit to the bottom of a squat and hang out there. Elbows push knees out.

Dad tip: Go only as deep as feels okay. Depth comes with time.

The Workout5/7
Move 04

Open book

8each side

Lie on your side, knees bent. Open your top arm across your body like turning a page. Follow it with your eyes.

Dad tip: Keep your knees stacked.

The Workout6/7
Move 05

Half-kneeling hip stretch

30 seceach side

One knee down, other foot forward. Squeeze the glute of the down leg and shift forward a little.

Dad tip: Pad the knee with a couch cushion.
