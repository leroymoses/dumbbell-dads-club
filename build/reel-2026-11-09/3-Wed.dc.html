Myth Busting2/6
The truth

2 days is the guideline

The CDC says adults need muscle-strengthening activity on at least 2 days a week, working all major muscle groups.

CDC, Physical Activity Guidelines: Adult Activity Overview

Translation: Two good nights beat seven guilty ones.
